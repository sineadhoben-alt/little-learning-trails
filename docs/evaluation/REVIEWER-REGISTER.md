## Private verification process

The owner asks teachers to describe their relevant NI KS2 subject teaching/curriculum experience and verifies it privately using an appropriate conversation or evidence already available to them. Shared register entries record only the broad scope, verification status and date; do not copy certificates, names or employers. “Self-declared” remains distinct from “owner verified.” Friendship alone is not evidence of subject expertise.

A teacher may approve both subjects only if their relevant expertise in each has been verified and they reviewed the stated scope. Primary/post-primary experience alone must not be relabelled as specific NI KS2 subject-specialist sign-off without checking. A parent's usability feedback does not become curriculum approval. Teacher and parent comments on appearance do not establish the credentials of an experienced children's educational-app artistic director.
