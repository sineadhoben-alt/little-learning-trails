# Supervised evaluation pack

**Status: prepared, not conducted.** The owner has identified teacher friends and parents as prospective reviewers who wish to remain anonymous. This pack does not confirm recruitment, consent, testing, approval or qualifications. Nobody has been contacted by Codex.

This is a practical evaluation of **Little Learning Trails — Maths & English**, for Northern Ireland P5–P7. It is not a test of a child, a diagnosis, a research ethics approval or evidence of educational effectiveness.

## Use in this order

1. The owner completes the participant information in [PARTICIPANT-PACK.md](PARTICIPANT-PACK.md), including contact method, storage location and deletion date. Keep consent records separate from shared results.
2. Freeze the candidate using the root release record. Enter its version, source hash and build identifiers on every session and review form. A source hash alone does not prove the installed app matches it.
3. Allocate reviewer codes in [REVIEWER-REGISTER.md](REVIEWER-REGISTER.md). Ask teachers to self-declare relevant experience; the owner verifies it privately. A parent's product feedback is valuable but does not replace subject-specialist approval.
4. Invite participants using the editable invitation. Obtain parent/guardian consent and the child's willing assent before a child session. Do not assume agreement because someone is a friend.
5. Run short supervised sessions using [OBSERVATION-FORM.md](OBSERVATION-FORM.md). Use fictional app nicknames and fresh test data. Aim for roughly 15–20 minutes, offer breaks and stop sooner if the child wishes or shows fatigue. Never finish the checklist at the child's expense.
6. Consolidate issues, prioritise fixes and record retests. Use codes in findings. Do not copy identifying work, screenshots or school records into the report.
7. Complete [SIGN-OFF.md](SIGN-OFF.md) only after its evidence requirements are satisfied. Re-open affected approvals after changes.

## Planned participation channels

The owner plans to seek Android feedback through a Google/Android testers community, online parent reviewers, and a limited group of iOS parents and children before release. These are **planned channels**, not recruited participants or completed tests. No community has been contacted through this pack and no community endorsement is claimed.

The owner coordinates invitations, access to the nominated beta build, private consent records and any child-session arrangements. Community adults can review installation, navigation and usability using fictional data without involving a child. A child session requires the same parent/guardian consent, willing assent and adult supervision as a local session; online recruitment does not waive those requirements. Do not contact children directly or request child photos, school information, recordings or public postings of their work. Use a private owner-managed feedback route instead of collecting identifying details in community comments.

Record the recruitment channel, actual platform/build and whether feedback came from an adult-only review or supervised child observation. iOS participation is currently planned to be limited; record that sampling limitation and do not extrapolate Android results to iOS. Parent and community usability reports are useful evidence but cannot substitute for verified NI KS2 English/Maths approval or professional artistic-direction/accessibility sign-off.

## Anonymity and minimum data

Shared reports use `T01…` for teachers, `P01…` for parents, `C01…` for children and `S01…` for sessions. These are templates, not allocated participants. Describe published feedback as **coded/pseudonymous** where the owner retains a private link; do not promise that it is completely anonymous to the owner. A name-free report can still identify someone through unusual details, so omit school, town, exact age, specific job title and distinctive personal information.

The owner keeps private consent/contact records and any code-link file separately with access restricted to those who need it. No participant names or signatures belong in the shared repository. Do not record audio/video, photograph children, collect diagnoses, upload child work or ask for real personal details in the app. The invitation and forms are practical templates, not a legal-compliance assurance.

## Completion criteria

- Each completed session links to a verified candidate/device and recorded consent/assent status without exposing names.
- Actual P5/P6/P7 coverage, access needs accommodated and missing groups are recorded.
- Comprehension, recovery, independence, appeal, fatigue and access are observed; assistance is logged.
- Findings distinguish observation, participant opinion and reviewer inference.
- Every release blocker is corrected and retested on the nominated release candidate.
- Human subject approvals, design/accessibility review and technical evidence are recorded against that exact candidate. Missing approval stays **pending**, not assumed.
