# Planned pre-release distribution

Owner plan recorded 26 September 2026: Android testers community and online parent reviewers, with a limited invited iOS parent/child group. No invitations, uploads, store-track setup or completed testing are recorded here.

## Android

Use an owner-controlled Google Play closed testing track for the intended cohort. A community recruitment channel is not Google endorsement or educational accreditation. Record actual opt-in, install, device/build and substantive feedback separately. Supply the genuine opt-in link only once the owner has created and checked the track; no placeholder store link should be presented as live.

For personal developer accounts created after 13 November 2023, Google currently requires at least 12 testers continuously opted in for 14 days before applying for production access. Confirm the owner's actual account requirements in Play Console; do not assume this condition applies or that meeting it proves educational quality. [Google account testing requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en), [test track setup](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334?hl=en-GB). Checked 26 September 2026.

## iOS

Use an invitation-only external TestFlight group for the limited cohort, with parent/guardian contact details held privately by the owner. The first external build needs TestFlight App Review and required beta information before invitations. The locally built simulator app is not a TestFlight upload or an installable iPhone distribution build. Signing, developer team, store record and approved external build remain pending. [Apple TestFlight](https://developer.apple.com/testflight/), [external tester invitations](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/invite-external-testers). Checked 26 September 2026.

## Evidence across both routes

Use the participant information, adult consent and child assent materials in this folder. An adult-only review is recorded as such. Child sessions are supervised by a parent or responsible adult; record actual P5/P6/P7 coverage and optional support preferences without requesting diagnoses. Participants may stop at any point. Do not publish children's names, work or images through community channels.

Use coded reports to respect reviewers' request for anonymity. Store private platform invitations, consent records and any reviewer-identity verification separately from shared feedback. Public anonymity does not remove the need for the owner to verify a curriculum reviewer's relevant expertise privately.

Ask each tester to record: build/device, activity or project ID, what they tried, expected/actual result, effect on the child, whether work survived leaving/reopening, any access barrier, and a suggested change. Remove personal details before sharing screenshots or logs. Keep Android and iOS results distinct, including large text, screen-reader, offline and interrupted-saving checks.

Community and parent evidence informs usability, reliability and appeal. Detailed NI KS2 subject approval and experienced artistic/inclusion review remain separate requirements. Resolve blockers, retest affected tasks, identify the final build and obtain explicit owner/reviewer decisions before public launch.
