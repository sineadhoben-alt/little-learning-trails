# Curriculum coverage register

Little Learning Trails — Maths & English · 26 September 2026

Sources: [CCEA KS2 Mathematics and Numeracy](https://ccea.org.uk/document/2066) and [CCEA KS2 Language and Literacy](https://ccea.org.uk/document/2054). Topic labels are paraphrases of the minimum-content statements. This register maps topic opportunities, not a validated assessment framework or evidence of pupil mastery.

88 topic statements map to 62 implemented trails, each with three tasks. Closed-answer logic and mathematical calculations have automated checks. Open work has adult guidance and reflection. Every individual activity awaits detailed educational review. Sinéad Hoben has approved the educational approach, drawing on primary and post-primary teaching experience.

## Activity objectives

- **The little market** (shop; Maths, Number · Money): Use money calculations and plan spending within a budget. Format: shop. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Build a pocket garden** (garden; Maths, Measures · Shape and Space): Construct rectangles and calculate their perimeter and area. Format: garden. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **The sharing patch** (fractions; Maths, Number · Fractions): Represent fractions and connect them to quantities and percentages. Format: fractions. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Wildlife watch** (data; Maths, Handling Data): Interpret charts, compare counts and calculate a mean. Format: data. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **The lighthouse mystery** (detective; English, Reading): Retrieve information and justify inferences with textual evidence. Format: detective. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **The word tinkerer** (words; English, Writing · Grammar and spelling): Use sentence structure, punctuation and spelling to communicate clearly. Format: words. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **The story studio** (story; English, Writing): Plan, compose and revise imaginative writing for an audience. Format: story. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Listen, imagine, share** (talk; English, Talking and Listening): Listen, give instructions and discuss ideas with reasons. Format: talk. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Number neighbourhood** (place-value; Maths, Number): Read, order and partition whole numbers and decimals. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **The number lift** (decimal-scaling; Maths, Number): Multiply and divide numbers by 10 and 100 using place value. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **The sensible answer** (estimate; Maths, Number): Estimate and round to judge whether a calculation is reasonable. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Below zero** (negative; Maths, Number): Use negative numbers in temperature and other contexts. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Pattern paths** (sequences; Maths, Number): Follow, explain and devise rules for number sequences. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Number connections** (factors; Maths, Number): Use multiples, factors, primes, squares, cubes and inverse operations. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Function factory** (machines; Maths, Number): Use function machines and generalise simple relationships. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **The missing number** (unknown; Maths, Number): Use a letter to represent an unknown number. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Clever calculation** (mental; Maths, Number): Choose mental addition and subtraction strategies. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Equal groups** (tables; Maths, Number): Recall multiplication facts to 10 × 10 and their related division facts. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Calculation workshop** (operations; Maths, Number): Use four operations, brackets and decimal calculations in problems. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Different names, same share** (fraction-links; Maths, Number): Connect fractions, decimals and percentages and use fractions of quantities. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Plan a class picnic** (budget; Maths, Number): Prioritise a budget, compare value and discuss safe ways to pay. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **A trip across the border** (currency; Maths, Number): Recognise pounds and euros and calculate with a stated example exchange rate. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Measure like a scientist** (measure-estimate; Maths, Measures): Estimate measures and choose units, instruments and appropriate accuracy. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Unit explorers** (units; Maths, Measures): Convert metric units and solve measurement problems. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Pack the parcel** (volume; Maths, Measures): Calculate volumes of simple cuboids using unit cubes. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Map makers** (scale; Maths, Measures): Interpret and create simple scale drawings. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Time travellers** (time; Maths, Measures): Read analogue and digital time, convert clock formats and use timetables. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Shape detectives** (shapes; Maths, Shape and Space): Construct and classify regular and irregular 2-D shapes by their properties. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Patterns in the tiles** (symmetry; Maths, Shape and Space): Explore reflection, line and rotational symmetry, and tessellation. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **From flat to solid** (solids; Maths, Shape and Space): Describe 3-D shapes and connect faces, edges, vertices and nets. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Compass code** (directions; Maths, Shape and Space): Use compass points, turns and simple programs to generate shapes. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Turn and discover** (angles; Maths, Shape and Space): Recognise, calculate, measure and draw angles up to a full turn. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Find the treasure** (coordinates; Maths, Shape and Space): Plot coordinates and draw shapes in the first quadrant. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Your own investigation** (collect-data; Maths, Handling Data): Plan an investigation, collect and classify data, and explain conclusions. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Read the record** (frequency; Maths, Handling Data): Interpret lists, tables and grouped frequency information. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **The data desk** (data-tools; Maths, Handling Data): Organise, query and interpret data using a spreadsheet or database. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **What is typical?** (averages; Maths, Handling Data): Calculate and interpret the mean and range of discrete data. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Could it happen?** (chance; Maths, Handling Data): Describe possible outcomes, likelihood, certainty, impossibility and evens. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Make a plan, test an idea** (reasoning; Maths, Processes in Mathematics): Select information and strategies, explain reasoning and check generalisations. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **The reading nook** (reading-library; English, Reading): Explore fiction, poetry and information texts using different reading purposes. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Find your next favourite** (reading-habits; English, Reading): Build sustained reading, preferences and shared or independent reading experiences. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Can I trust this?** (source-detective; English, Reading): Locate, select and evaluate information for a purpose across sources. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **One event, different voices** (media; English, Reading): Compare how texts and media present information and engage attention. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Show what you understood** (reading-response; English, Reading): Represent and discuss understanding through visual, spoken, dramatic and digital responses. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Unlock an unfamiliar word** (word-strategies; English, Reading): Combine decoding, word parts and context, then cross-check meaning. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Choose your reading tool** (reading-purpose; English, Reading): Skim, scan and read closely for different purposes. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Listen for the meaning** (listen-texts; English, Talking and Listening): Listen and respond to stories, poems, drama and media messages. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Step into the story** (retell-drama; English, Talking and Listening): Retell, interpret and improvise stories and real or imagined experiences. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Every voice matters** (discussion; English, Talking and Listening): Use discussion conventions, reasons and evidence to share and evaluate ideas. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Say it clearly** (instructions; English, Talking and Listening): Give, follow and improve guidance and directions. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Your idea, your audience** (presentations; English, Talking and Listening): Prepare oral presentations and adapt voice and media for a familiar audience. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **The curious interviewer** (questions; English, Talking and Listening): Ask suitable questions and talk in formal and informal situations. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Words in different voices** (voice; English, Talking and Listening): Explore expressive reading, register, dialect and colloquial language. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Write to make a difference** (factual-writing; English, Writing): Plan and compose factual writing, opinions and persuasive texts for different audiences. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Make the page work** (layout; English, Writing): Choose text forms and layouts appropriate to a purpose and audience. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **The poetry playground** (poetry; English, Writing): Experiment with rhyme, rhythm, verse, wordplay and dialect. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Make the reader feel it** (writers-style; English, Writing): Develop stylistic choices, personal voice, mood and effect through revision. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Words, pictures, meaning** (digital-composition; English, Writing): Organise and present ideas using digital text with graphics or sound. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **From conversation to page** (spoken-written; English, Writing): Recognise differences between spoken and written language. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Word-building workshop** (spelling; English, Writing): Use sound, word parts, word families and checking strategies to spell. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **Punctuation with a purpose** (grammar; English, Writing): Use grammar and punctuation to make meaning clear. Format: practice. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.
- **A message worth reading** (handwriting; English, Writing): Develop comfortable, fluent and legible handwriting. Format: practical. Three levels implemented; teacher review pending.

## Remaining depth and validation

Calibrated diagnostics, broader longitudinal practice evidence and qualified teacher validation remain outstanding. Basic saved charts, coordinate plots and labelled compositions are included; a full spreadsheet engine, media editor and publishing portfolio are not. Practical tasks currently ask learners to use paper, real objects, a partner or a separate tool where specified. Coverage of a topic does not mean every possible skill within it has sufficient practice.
