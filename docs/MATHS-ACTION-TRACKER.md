## Re-review responsibilities

- **NI KS2 Maths specialist:** check all supplied models, subskill progression, representations, assumptions and curriculum adequacy. Confirm optional extension work is clearly separated from core expectations.
- **Classroom teacher and inclusion specialist:** trial paper/tool activities, resource access, reading demand, practical support and alternative response methods with appropriate consent.
- **Product reviewer:** confirm every project opens directly, retains its source data and saved learner evidence, and remains available after reopening. Verify table/chart and coordinate tools against their claims.
- **Owner:** approve beta scope wording after these findings and tests; the earlier teacher approval of the approach is not automatic sign-off on these newly authored resources.
