# Maths beta teaching resources and practice

26 September 2026 · AI-assisted authoring. Qualified NI KS2 teacher validation and supervised pupil trials remain pending. These are implemented source resources, not independent educational approval.

## Added

- All **35 Maths trails** now have explicit prerequisites and three named teaching steps: **105 original worked examples**, with reasoning and task-specific support/developing/secure criteria. Examples are instruction and must not count as unseen assessment.
- **561 additional practice items** across **72 activity-level keys**, at least four per bank. Each preserves the target subskill and the interactive task fields. The original questions remain available.
- Multiplication banks plus the original tasks include every ordered product with factors **1 through 12** and multipliers **1 through 12**, plus 144 corresponding inverse division questions. Tables are grouped into familiar facts, other products and inverse division; those are different subskills, not calibrated ability bands.
- Explicitly duplicated original fraction, scaling, lift, equation and table items were avoided in the generated variants. Original plus practice identity must be used by the app; repeated display of one task must not count as new evidence.
- Corrected self-contained spreadsheet instructions, whole-minute grouped-data wording, one-choice-per-child wording, exact unit-cube filling and fair-selection assumptions in the original Maths content.
- **47 self-contained guided curriculum investigations** add resource selection, information gathering, retained explanations, spatial generalisation, whole-number sequences/reading/ordering, broader calculations, area/temperature estimates, precision, multi-entry timetables, common solids, protractor instruction, grouped frequencies, outlier interpretation and fair trials. ## Integration and remaining limits

The app must save the selected practice item and its draft together. A fixed demonstration diagram must not be presented as the current variant’s data. Guided work should retain notes and before/after versions, present materials before starting, and expose adult criteria without claiming the checklist proves mastery. The app may offer a table/chart, coordinate plotting and a saved explanation; physical measurement and safe paper construction remain explicitly identified activities.

The data-desk project distinguishes the app’s editable table from a spreadsheet formula engine. Dedicated pictogram and line-chart rendering are not claimed: the project provides concrete interpretation data and invites a drawn/recorded explanation. Area/temperature readings can be clearly marked simulated where equipment is unavailable. MN2 includes optional smaller-than-hundredth extension values; the core KS2 path should remain centred on the required two-decimal work.

The review’s “next action” resources are authored for every Maths row; sustained practice sufficiency, developmental progression, accessibility in real use and long-term recall cannot be certified by this implementation. Human Maths specialist sign-off must follow review of the new tasks and learner evidence.

## Times-table extension

Following the owner’s question, 11 and 12 tables and related divisions were appended without reordering existing saved question indices. NI minimum content names 10×10; England specifies 12×12. The app includes the larger range as additional practice, with 10-plus-1 and 10-plus-2 strategies.
